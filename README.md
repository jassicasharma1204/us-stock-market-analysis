US stock market analysis dashboard built in Microsoft Excel


📊 **Project : US Stock Market Analysis Dashboard in Excel**

I'm excited to share my stock market analysis project, where I used Microsoft Excel to turn raw market data into a structured dashboard.

**What I worked on:**
- Built KPI cards for total trading volume, average closing price, and record count.
- Created PivotTables and charts to compare trading volume, average closing prices, and daily price ranges.
- Analyzed monthly trading volume patterns.
- Calculated daily range and daily return metrics to explore stock price movements.
- Worked on dashboard design and interactive filtering using slicers and a date timeline.

**Some observations from my dashboard:**
- Symbol `C` has the highest total trading volume among the symbols displayed in my Top 10 chart.
- Trading volume increases from August to September in the displayed period, before declining through November.
- `GOOG` has the highest average closing price among the symbols displayed in my ranking chart.

This project helped me practise data summarization, PivotTables, chart selection, calculated metrics, and dashboard design in Excel.
